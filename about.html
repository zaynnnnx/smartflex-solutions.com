Built to be the flexible alternative in registry and fund services

SmartFlex Solutions was founded on a simple idea: issuers and fund managers deserve a partner that adapts to them, not a one-size-fits-all platform.

Our Story

From a single registry desk to a six-office global practice

SmartFlex Solutions was established in London in 2011 by a small team of registry and fund operations specialists who believed that shareholder services had grown too rigid, too slow, and too disconnected from the companies they were meant to serve.

What started as a boutique transfer agency for early-stage issuers has grown into a global practice spanning transfer agency, shareholder registry, investment fund administration, public company support and IPO advisory — without losing the responsiveness that made us different in the first place.

Today, SmartFlex supports more than 500 corporate and fund clients across six international offices, administering upwards of $42 billion in assets while keeping every relationship personal, direct and genuinely flexible.

What Drives Us

Our mission, vision and values

Our Mission

To give every issuer and fund manager — regardless of size — access to registry and fund infrastructure that flexes around their business, not the other way around.

Our Vision

To be recognised globally as the registry and fund administration partner companies choose when they want a genuine relationship, not just a service ticket.

Our Values

Integrity in every register we hold, precision in every calculation we run, and flexibility in every relationship we build.
