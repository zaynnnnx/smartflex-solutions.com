Registry, fund and listing services that flex around your business

Modular, jurisdiction-aware services for issuers, fund managers and companies preparing to go public — delivered by dedicated teams, not ticket queues.

Core Service

Transfer Agency & Shareholder Registry

We maintain accurate, secure shareholder registers for issuers of every size — strengthening issuer-shareholder trust with infrastructure built to scale across jurisdictions.

  • Full register maintenance, transfers and certificate issuance
  • Corporate actions: dividends, splits, rights issues and buybacks
  • AGM/EGM notice distribution, proxy voting and tabulation
  • Unclaimed property and escheatment compliance
Core Service

Investment Fund Administration

We support investment managers with the global distribution and administration of mutual funds, ETFs, hedge funds and alternative investment vehicles.

  • NAV calculation, fund accounting and reconciliation
  • Investor onboarding, KYC/AML and subscription processing
  • Distribution support across multiple regulatory regimes
  • Investor reporting portals with real-time statements
Core Service

Public Company Support

We act as registrar and transfer agent for companies listed on leading global exchanges, including the LSE, NYSE, HKEX, TSE, SGX and ASX.

  • Multi-exchange registrar and transfer agent services
  • Employee share plan and equity compensation administration
  • Regulatory filing coordination and disclosure support
  • Shareholder analytics and beneficial ownership tracking
Core Service

IPO & Listing Advisory

For companies preparing to enter public markets, we provide the transfer agency framework critical to a successful listing — from underwriter coordination to first AGM.

  • Pre-IPO share structure and cap table preparation
  • Underwriter and exchange coordination through pricing and listing day
  • Post-listing shareholder administration and communications
  • First AGM planning, proxy setup and investor relations support
